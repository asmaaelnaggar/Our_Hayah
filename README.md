# 🩸 Hayah – Blood Donation Platform

**Hayah** is a free web application designed to connect people who are willing to donate blood with patients and people in need of blood donations.

The main goal of the project is to make the process of finding a suitable blood donor easier, faster, and more accessible, while encouraging people to participate in blood donation and help others in need.

## Deployment 
https://website-hayah.vercel.app/

## 💡 Project Idea

Finding a blood donor in an urgent situation can be difficult and time-consuming.

**Hayah** provides a simple platform where:

* 🩸 Blood donors can register and provide their blood type and information.
* 🔍 People in need can search for suitable donors based on their blood type.
* 🤝 The platform helps connect donors with people who need blood.
* 💙 The service is completely free and focused on helping people and encouraging blood donation.

## 🎯 Project Goals
